Our Story

Rooted in the kitchen we grew up in

Bhumik began at a wood stove in a small courtyard kitchen, where every meal was measured in patience, not minutes. Today, we still grind our spices fresh each morning and simmer our curries for hours — because a shortcut kitchen was never the plan.

Every dish that leaves our tandoor carries a story of the hands that shaped it, from our head chef's grandmother's recipe book to the plate in front of you.
